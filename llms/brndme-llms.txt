# BRND.ME

> BRND.ME (legally Mensa Brand Technologies Private Limited) is an India-based house-of-brands company that acquires, builds, and scales digital-first consumer brands across Health, Wellness, Lifestyle, Fashion, Party & Celebration, and Home Decor. Founded by Ananth Narayanan and backed by Accel, it uses technology, data, and centralized operations to grow market-leading D2C brands from India for global customers. BRND.ME operates a corporate marketing presence and does not publish a public developer API.
